How it works

Three simple steps to recover your lost item

1
Report your item
Fill in a quick form with details — name, category, description, location, and date. Both lost and found items can be reported.
2
Automatic matching
Our system compares reports using fuzzy matching and similarity scoring. Matches above 80% are surfaced automatically.
3
Reunite & close
Review the match, confirm it, and coordinate return. Item status updates automatically from Pending → Verified → Returned.
